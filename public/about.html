Oshskyle Logistics Private Limited is an international freight forwarding and customs clearing company established in Bengaluru, India — with its own set-up and a wide network of agents across the world.

Why choose us

Door to door.
End to end.

We provide door-to-door logistics solutions that are critical to the success of the companies and people we serve — the reason clients count us among the best freight forwarders in India.

Our team of experienced, knowledgeable professionals is dedicated to the highest level of service and support, ensuring goods are delivered on time and in good condition. Our focus is on optimising your supply chain, reducing costs, and improving efficiency.

Mission

To provide reliable, efficient, and innovative logistics solutions that exceed customer expectations. By leveraging cutting-edge technology, expert services, and a commitment to excellence, we ensure the safe, timely, and cost-effective movement of goods across borders.

Vision

To become a global leader in logistics, known for our integrity, professionalism, and exceptional service — a world where businesses can rely on Oshskyle for their end-to-end logistics needs, fostering growth, efficiency, and success across industries worldwide.
